# Delivery-observed application panel planner V3

Purpose: prepare paired, actual-application panels that explicitly bind
`paced_application_cell_v2.py`, its source-delivery policy and its distinct
successful-closure status. This is a new immutable planner version; existing
V1/V2 plans, runners, qualifications and source releases remain unchanged.

Inputs are complete reviewed main (7,680) and modes-panel (1,536) comparison
banks, an evidence-based selection of the baseline plus at most five alternatives,
the frozen 480 saved-audio jobs, fixed 24-case panel and eight timing anchors,
accepted component source/runtime/model bindings, and the qualified complete
journal application derivative. The development probe does not require completed
comparison banks; its explicit unavailable-review fixtures cannot pass the
production reconstruction gate and do not produce a real shortlist or plan.

V3 rechecks DELIVERY_APPLICATION_PRESTART_CHECK_V1.json, its exact stopped helper,
child and publisher identities, recorded native closure and all 16 actual backend
preparation/closure receipts. Exact routes, job, runtime, gallery, source class
hashes, delivery policy, Controller closure and never-started captures are required.
The recorded unobserved native process assignment remains an explicit limitation;
prestart verification grants no resource, model, timing or continuity acceptance.
Private proof files are confined to their own tree, reject reparse paths and have
a 2-MiB per-file read limit. Every binding is verified before and after reading.

The application policy is included in the context hashed by every row cache key.
A V3 execution request rejects an older schema, missing policy, changed variant,
success status or corrected timing. Its child payload keeps the existing strict
allowlist: audio-only job, backend contract, actual source/catalog/gallery/runtime
and model bindings, and unchanged execution policy. Truth, component reports,
selection rationale and the parent-only application policy are not passed to the
inference child. A future qualified V3 runner must use the explicit policy and new
cell status; this planner does not authorize or launch a source.

Outputs in a fresh private directory are PROBE_OWNER.json, source snapshots,
ADMISSION.json, tests.txt, and RESULT.json or FAILED.json for development; production
preparation writes ADMISSION.json, PLAN.json and RESULT.json. Each chosen candidate
has 24 panel cases plus two extra occurrences of each of eight timing anchors,
40 cells total. Plans carry zero N4 acceptance and no continuity credit.

The guarded probe runs 11 regression tests plus 1,240 development payloads across
all 16 real catalog routes. It hashes saved inputs without executing audio, checks
active D1 ownership/source bindings, and runs on CPU14 BelowNormal with one math
thread and GPU disabled. It uses the existing helper lock, 720-second limit,
8-MiB output guard, C:50/G:75 GiB free-space floors, packaging cutoff and shared
50-GiB allowance including 6 GiB reserve. It never creates a model worker, app,
visible window or connection to the Pi. This phase-specific probe requires a
healthy D1 run; if the phase changes, re-admit a fresh derivative rather than
weakening that check. Failed attempts and their source snapshots are preserved.
