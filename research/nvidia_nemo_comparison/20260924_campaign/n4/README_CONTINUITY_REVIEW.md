# Selected continuity evidence review

Purpose: independently review every selected backend's stopped full-file
continuity evidence. `review_continuity_transport.py` binds the exact
`continuity_application_runner.py` identity and reconstructs native and delivery
envelopes. `review_continuity_cell.py` joins these to source/consumer clocks,
complete engine and archive closure, resource ownership and viewport observations.
`review_continuity_application.py` reconstructs the qualified plan and runner,
then checks every selected candidate and progress receipt before reviewing cells.
All earlier qualified code remains unchanged. No process is launched, signalled,
terminated or replayed by these readers, and no Pi connection occurs.

The production input is a **stopped** `continuity_application_runner.py` output
directory. Its exact preparer and coordinator identities must have exited.
Its plan must reconstruct through `continuity_application_plan.admit_plan`,
including full main/modes score-review selection, application lineage and exact
prepared PCM. Expected population comes from that plan: baseline plus zero to
five selected alternatives, in exact order, each receiving the identical O0
20:06.78 full file with no internal resets. Short-panel schemas, partial/duplicate/
extra/missing cells or progress entries, reordered candidates, substituted audio,
changed code, unclean closure, incomplete native journals, rebound false delivery
summaries and unexpected source-session origins fail review.

Outputs in a fresh private directory: REVIEW_OWNER.json, ADMISSION.json,
cells/0001.json etc., and REVIEW.json (`PASS_COMPLETE_CONTINUITY_EVIDENCE_COVERAGE_ONLY`)
or a preserved FAILED.json. Original application evidence is never modified.
Compact results retain every independently verified input binding, full-reader
fingerprint, publication/source census, raw delivery summary and source clock.
Complete source delivery and one source-start publication must join the native
journal, consumer and viewport origin and finish before recorded engine closure.
Append/scheduling costs are not subtracted. Exact lease renewal history and all
short-lived process identities cannot be reconstructed and remain explicit limits.

This review checks coverage and consistency of uninterrupted source evidence.
It does **not** grant timing deadlines, model accuracy, semantic captions/names,
resource thresholds, functional mid-file stop/restart, deployment tiers or N4
acceptance. Those flags remain false. Functional restart is a separate required
test in the same Controller/UI. One new process per candidate does not prove it.
Internal readers also accept short synthetic fixtures for development; only the
production entry point enforces the exact full-length continuity plan/population.
