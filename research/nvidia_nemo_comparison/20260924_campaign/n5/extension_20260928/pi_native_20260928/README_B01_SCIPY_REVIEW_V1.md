saved-file runs. No model inference is performed by this reader. Requires exact
admission/source hashes and closed boot/PID/start-tick owners; natural systemd0;
complete source/ASR/D1 coverage; contiguous probabilities equal to the prior same
source delayed reference (max absolute1e-5); finite normalized192D E0 vectors with
unchanged0.5–2s contiguous windows; no names from the empty gallery; caption passage,
zero inference failures and drained application/archive queues and handles.

Outputs: private REVIEW_INPUTS.json (includes vectors/raw evidence) and REVIEW.json
on host; REVIEW.json also on target. Success is explicitly E0_REFERENCE_PENDING:
this reader does not independently recompute E0 arrays or qualify real-life quality,
