Opening stays idle with capture off. Choose one of six backend combinations,
Live microphone or Saved WAV, then Open Application and a Mode. Eleven Mode
policies are visible; backend, identity policy and calibration are separate.

Normal live uses manual Stop and storage capacity without arbitrary duration,
recording, people, reference or slot counts, or a fixed ordinary backlog cutoff.
Actual memory/AS/free-space, finite capacity-derived file/drain allowances,
bounded queues, source/lease/owner, I/O and cleanup guards remain. Individual
lane delay labels are unavailable; aggregate backlog remains in health metadata.
Model geometry, thresholds and calibration math are unchanged.

1. Open the Just Peachy shortcut. Choose one of the six backend/encoder rows and
   Live microphone or Saved WAV, then Open Application.
2. The portrait app opens idle. Select Mode and matching/display rosters.
   Use People for deliberate compatible enrollment; ReDimNet and TitaNet have
   separate references. Opening the app does not begin listening.
3. Press Start. Read current captions; pending text can revise, and delayed
   labels may update the same source span. Scrolling freezes its parent window;
   use Return to live to resume the current tail.
4. Press Stop and wait for drain/source/model/worker closure.
5. Save the processed session or deliberately Discard its entire temporary
   UUID. Qualified raw requires the separately admitted four physical-channel
   route. A discarded microphone check is not evidence of raw Save.
6. Reopen/export a kept session in paged History, or deliberately Delete it.
   Settings' Return to backend combinations closes the controller and returns
   idle to the chooser. Discard itself does not perform that transition.

Read [Mode guide](MODE_GUIDE.md) for the eleven visible policies and
[backend combinations](BACKEND_COMBINATIONS.md) for the six rows. Unknown can be
a valid naming decision: enrollment is not calibration, cosine is not a
probability, and closed-group/seat display assumptions are not verified names.

Activated build35 uses physical capacity for total recording/gallery contents;
arbitrary count/duration/cumulative-metadata and inherited gallery ceilings
are removed in the reviewed current source. Bounded queues, files, payloads and ownership
checks remain. Normal live has no fixed backlog cutoff; real I/O, source/queue-health and hardware faults can stop a session. The known
reserve+8MiB writable-SQLite edge can block Discard before its first unlink;
see [recovery](INSTALL_HEALTH_AND_RECOVERY.md).
