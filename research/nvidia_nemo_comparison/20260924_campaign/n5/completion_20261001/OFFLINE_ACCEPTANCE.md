## Physical field check, once software acceptance passes

Before taking the Pi out, the operator disconnects Ethernet, confirms the actual offline indicator/state, opens the installed launcher, checks touchscreen orientation/controls, performs a short consented session, Stops, Saves, Reopens and confirms local persistence. Reboot while disconnected is a separate observation. Record each action in a field run; no remote engineer should claim physical actions without evidence.

The final acceptance document must distinguish: asset-complete; process-network-isolated; device-network-disabled; physical-cable-disconnected; cold-boot-offline; visible GUI; physical touch; function; sustained resources; real-world quality. These are independent claims.
