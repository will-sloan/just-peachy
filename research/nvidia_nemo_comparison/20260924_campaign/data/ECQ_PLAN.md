# N1 E/C/Q reference plan

The local audit independently verified 385 clean enrollment (E) clips, 367 clean calibration (C) clips and 449 unique query (Q) waveforms underlying all 777 source utterance occurrences. Source IDs, original file hashes, decoded PCM hashes, prompt groups and native prompt keys have empty pairwise E/C/Q intersections. The original audit also declares book/chapter lineage exclusions; three HiFiTTS people have a declared same-book E/C fallback with different chapters. Undocumented codec/crop aliases and unique-human re-identification are not exhaustively ruled out.

The 43-person clean-source capability matrix retains all 5/15/30-second requested tiers, including honest insufficient-duration cases. Whole clips and estimated active duration are recorded separately. Prefix tiers count unique source material; repeating one clip or playing it at two positions does not add enrollment duration. Keep unavailable people/tiers unavailable instead of borrowing query speech.

Existing accepted S6D enrollment captures provide 30 people, two recorded positions per person, and six processed mono streams per position: 360 hash-verified waveform conditions. They use disjoint existing E material. Prefer the `auto_asr_raw` and `auto_pp_raw` streams for the primary processed-domain enrollment comparison; preserve position and stream as separate conditions. These are the same XVF device family, but their saved S6D configuration and room transfer are not identical to every S4.5 query configuration. The other four fixed-focus streams remain declared alternatives, without being silently pooled into the primary condition.

Clean E is a separate, explicitly labelled source-domain condition. Clean C is available for compatible score/margin calibration, with its cross-domain limitation declared when used against processed E/Q. Existing C embeddings are ReDimNet-specific evidence and must not be loaded into TitaNet. Each candidate must freshly extract both E templates and C scores from exactly the same permitted waveform spans and its own model/configuration hash. Profile UUIDs can remain stable while vectors and calibration keys remain model-specific. A score or margin is not a calibrated percentage.

The supplemental audit also located and hash-verified 12 accepted existing processed C captures, with 72 mono stream conditions and 30 disjoint C source clips. Their original admission is explicitly collection-only: exact gold-time projection and selector thresholds were not admitted. They remain available as saved same-device evidence, but are not silently treated as ready calibrated per-speaker segments. Any future use for threshold fitting needs a separate C-only source-to-output projection review that preserves the saved uncertainty; Q never participates. `PROCESSED_C_AUDIT.json` and the private bindings retain that restriction.

Use the fixed S6C rosters as the principal independent roster conditions. The local `ECQ_BINDINGS.json` retains their source definitions. The versioned `ENROLLMENT_ROSTER_PLAN.json` freezes 84 conditions across clean 5/15/30-second and processed 15-second E: three existing roster families at requested sizes 0, 1, 2, 4, 8, 15, and all original members. For reduced sizes, round-robin the existing per-corpus seed order in sorted corpus-name order, then take deterministic prefixes. This interleaving and membership freeze use no model outputs or scene truth. Retain intended and actually available membership separately; unsupported people remain explicitly unavailable instead of being replaced. The plan counts known-reference strangers and absent enrolled people across all 240 queries. All queries stay in scope, even when their speakers lack sufficient E material. Closed-roster mode declares a user assumption; it must not turn an unknown into evidence of recognition. Scene-derived roster diagnostic conditions must be explicitly labelled and never used to report independent open-world recognition.

No personal profile directory or private personal voice recording was opened by this audit. The existing personal gallery stays intact. Research galleries remain outside Git and are never shipped as personal profiles. Enrollment persistence/interface tests must use isolated synthetic metadata or these already-authorized research references. Neural enrollment comparisons occur in later authorized stages; N1 does not rank models.

Admission requirements for each future candidate are: exact audio bindings, domain and position, E/C/Q role, unique duration, compatible encoder hash, model-specific vector dimension, permitted source rights, and an empty reference/query overlap check. Re-extraction is required whenever the encoder, window, preprocessing, precision or streaming state changes.
