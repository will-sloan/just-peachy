## Use and limits

The app starts offline using local models. The retained rc2-directory launcher
selects the activated current release; its path is not the app version.
Native XVF I2S/I2C remains on the 40-pin link; no XVF micro-USB is required.

BMI270 has no absolute heading/position reference. Gravity stabilizes tilt,
not yaw; drift and unmeasured turns cannot be eliminated. Translation can
change the actual speaker angle. Linear-array front/back and source-elevation
ambiguities remain. Indefinite direction retention cannot be guaranteed.
